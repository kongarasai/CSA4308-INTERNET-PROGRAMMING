Empowering Citizens for a Smarter, Safer City

CivicConnect replaces slow paper processes with instant transparent grievance redressal. Report potholes, garbage overflow, broken streetlights, or water leaks with automatic SOAP-based municipal department routing.

Supported Municipal Issue Categories

🛣️ Roads & Potholes

Report hazardous road cracks, missing manhole covers, and deep potholes impacting commuter safety.

Auto-routed to Roads Dept SLA: 24h
🧹 Sanitation & Trash

Report overflowing community garbage dumpsters, uncollected waste, or illegal dumping sites.

Auto-routed to Sanitation SLA: 12h
💡 Street Lighting

Report dark streets, non-functional lighting fixtures, or dangerous exposed electrical wires.

Auto-routed to Electricity SLA: 36h
🚰 Water & Sewerage

Report high pressure main supply pipe bursts, sewage blockage, or contaminated drinking water.

Auto-routed to Water Board SLA: 18h

⚙️ SOA Architecture & SOAP Integration

CivicConnect integrates a JAX-WS SOAP Web Service (DepartmentAllocationService) described by WSDL contract to handle rule-based real-time department routing based on complaint categories.

  • Automated SLA estimation & priority assignment
  • XSD XML Schema validation for structured payload integrity
  • XSLT XML transformation for departmental reporting
